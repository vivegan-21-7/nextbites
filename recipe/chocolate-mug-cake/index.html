Chocolate Mug Cake

Warm, gooey single-serve cake from the microwave.

⏱ 5 minEasyDessert

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 3 tbsp milk & 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tbsp chocolate chips

Method

  1. Whisk dry ingredients directly inside a microwave-safe mug.
  2. Add milk and melted butter; mix until a smooth batter forms.
  3. Drop chocolate chips into the center of the batter.
  4. Microwave on high for 70 to 90 seconds.
  5. Let cool for 1 minute and enjoy warm.
