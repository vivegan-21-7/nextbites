Greek Salad

Crisp cucumber, tomato, olives and feta in olive oil.

⏱ 15 minEasyMediterranean

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 English cucumber, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
  • 100g block feta cheese
  • Extra virgin olive oil, dried oregano, lemon juice

Method

  1. Combine tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and olives in a shallow bowl.
  2. Place the feta cheese slab or cubes on top.
  3. Drizzle generously with extra virgin olive oil and a squeeze of lemon juice.
  4. Season with dried oregano and freshly cracked black pepper.
  5. Toss gently just before serving with crusty bread.
