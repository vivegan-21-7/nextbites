Masala Fries

Golden fries tossed in chaat masala and chilli.

⏱ 25 minEasySnacks

Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes, cut into fries
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste and fresh chopped coriander

Method

  1. Soak cut potatoes in cold water for 15 minutes, then pat dry thoroughly.
  2. Toss with oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Bake or air-fry at 200°C (400°F) until golden and crispy.
  4. Transfer to a bowl and immediately toss with chaat masala and chilli powder.
  5. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve with ketchup.
