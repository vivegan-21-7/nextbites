Lemon Iced Tea

Bright, fizzy, lightly sweet, perfect for a hot day.

⏱ 15 minEasyDrinks

Ingredients

  • 2 black tea bags
  • 2 cups hot water
  • 2 tbsp honey or sugar
  • Juice of 1 fresh lemon
  • Ice cubes and fresh mint sprigs

Method

  1. Steep tea bags in hot water for 4–5 minutes, then discard bags.
  2. Stir in honey or sugar until completely dissolved.
  3. Add freshly squeezed lemon juice and chill.
  4. Fill glasses with ice cubes and pour tea over.
  5. Garnish with lemon slices and mint leaves.
