Paneer Tikka

Smoky, spiced paneer cubes grilled with peppers and onion.

⏱ 40 minMediumIndian

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 1 bell pepper & 1 onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp tikka masala & kasuri methi

Method

  1. Whisk yogurt with spices, ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice.
  2. Coat paneer, peppers, and onions in the marinade for 20 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers alternating paneer and vegetables.
  4. Grill or roast hot until edges are charred and fragrant.
  5. Serve hot with mint chutney and lemon wedges.
