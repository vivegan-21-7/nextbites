Cold Brew Coffee

Smooth, low-acid coffee steeped slow overnight.

⏱ 10 minEasyDrinks

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coarsely ground coffee
  • 4 cups cold filtered water
  • Ice cubes
  • Milk or sweetener to taste

Method

  1. Combine coarse coffee grounds and cold water in a large jar.
  2. Stir gently to make sure all grounds are saturated.
  3. Cover and steep in the fridge for 12 to 18 hours.
  4. Strain through a fine mesh sieve or paper filter.
  5. Pour over ice and serve black or with a splash of milk.
